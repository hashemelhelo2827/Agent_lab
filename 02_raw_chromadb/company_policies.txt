### Vacation Policy
Employees get 20 paid vacation days per year. Must be approved by manager at least 2 weeks in advance.

### Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Team leads must be in-office on Tuesdays.

### Code of Conduct
Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited. Report violations to HR immediately.

### Equipment Policy
Laptops and monitors are provided on day one. Lost equipment costs $500 deductible.
